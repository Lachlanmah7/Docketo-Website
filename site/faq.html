Frequently asked questions

Questions about Docketo.

Do I need an account?

No. Docketo works locally without an account. Sign in when you want to use account features, including Premium Cloud Sync.

Where are my purchases stored?

Without Cloud Sync, purchase records and receipt images stay in app-controlled storage on your phone. With Premium Cloud Sync enabled, eligible records can also be available through your signed-in Docketo account.

Does Docketo back up automatically?

No. Backup export is user-initiated. You choose when and where to save the backup.

How do I move to a new phone?

Create a password-protected backup on the old phone, save it somewhere you can reach from the new phone, then restore it in Docketo. See the Backup and restore guide for the steps.

Why does the Free version have ads?

Advertising helps support continued development while keeping the core app available without an upfront purchase. Premium removes dashboard advertising while the subscription is active.

Does Premium include Cloud Sync?

Yes. Premium gives signed-in users access to optional Cloud Sync. Turn it on in Settings, then use Sync now when you want to update another device. See the Cloud Sync guide.

How do warranty reminders work?

Docketo schedules local notifications on the device. Notification delivery depends on Android notification permissions and device battery or background settings.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Individual purchases can be deleted, all local data can be removed from Settings, and signed-in users can delete their authentication account separately.

Is Docketo for children?

No. Docketo is not directed to children under 13.
