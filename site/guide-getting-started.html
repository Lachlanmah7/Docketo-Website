Detailed guide

Get started with Docketo.

Set up Docketo in a few minutes, then record the purchases and documents you want to keep safe.

All guidesNext: purchases and warranties
Step 1

Choose how you want to use Docketo.

When you first open Docketo, you can continue as a guest or sign in. Guest mode is ideal if you simply want to store records on this phone. It does not require an account.

Choose email sign-in if you want a Docketo account. Enter your name, email and a strong password, then open the verification email that Docketo sends. You can also choose Google sign-in and select the Google account you want to use.

Which option is right for me?

  • Guest: local records on one device
  • Email account: an account with email verification and password recovery
  • Google: a quicker sign-in using your Google account
  • Premium + account: required before using Cloud Sync

Step 2: Get familiar with the app

The dashboard gives you a quick snapshot of your saved products, warranty status and recent purchases. Your purchase list is where you search, filter and open saved items. Settings is where you control backups, reminders, account choices, Premium and Cloud Sync.

Step 2

Start with one useful record.

Do not try to enter everything at once. Add the latest appliance, phone, laptop or other purchase where a receipt and warranty could matter. Once you see the information together, adding the rest becomes much easier.

Step 3

Add your first purchase.

From your purchase list, select the option to add a purchase. Enter a clear product name first. Add the brand, store, price, purchase date, category, serial number and notes when they are useful.

Save the item. You can return to it anytime to edit details or add more documents.

Good first-record checklist

  • A name you will recognise later
  • Purchase date and store
  • Receipt image
  • Warranty length
  • Serial number for valuable electronics
You are ready
