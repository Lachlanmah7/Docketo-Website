Product roadmap

Where Docketo is heading.

The roadmap separates completed features from planned work so users and testers can see what exists today.

Current foundation

Purchase records, receipts, warranty tracking, notifications, backup and restore, authentication, privacy controls, ads and Premium subscription foundation.

Production readiness

Play Store testing, release signing, public privacy and deletion pages, subscription verification, policy review and closed testing.

Premium cloud services

Optional cloud sync, cloud receipt storage, multi-device access, entitlement-linked accounts and cloud deletion workflows.

Advanced organisation

Improved reporting, export options, household use, insurance-oriented inventory tools and more flexible categorisation.

Possible future tools

Barcode scanning, OCR-assisted entry, biometric app lock and other features subject to privacy, cost and maintenance review.
