1. Add and manage purchases

Keep the details you normally lose.

From the main purchase list, choose the option to add a purchase. Give the item a clear name, then add as much detail as is useful: brand, store, category, price, purchase date, serial number and notes.

Open any saved item later to edit it, view its receipt images, or remove it if you no longer need the record.

Helpful habits

  • Add an item as soon as you buy it
  • Use a name you will recognise in search
  • Save serial numbers for electronics and appliances
  • Use notes for return conditions or repair details
  • Use search and warranty filters when your list grows

Receipts and documents

While adding or editing a purchase, attach receipt images from your camera or photo library. You can attach supporting documents too, then open them from the saved purchase when you need them.

For best results, make sure the store name, date, total and product details are readable before you save the image.

2. Save the proof

Your receipt belongs with the purchase.

A receipt is often the part you need most for a return, warranty claim or insurance question. Docketo keeps it beside the item rather than buried in photos, email or a paper drawer.

3. Track warranties and reminders

Know what is covered before it is too late.

Set the warranty length while creating or editing a purchase. Docketo shows whether it is active, approaching expiry or expired. Use the warranty filters to see what needs attention.

Set a local reminder for an important warranty. If you do not receive it, check that notifications are allowed for Docketo in your phone’s settings.

Before a warranty expires

  • Open the purchase and review its receipt and notes
  • Check the product’s condition
  • Contact the retailer or manufacturer while the warranty is active
  • Keep any repair correspondence in your notes
4. Sign in when it helps

You can start without an account.

Local use does not require an account. Sign in only when you want to use the account-linked options available in the app.

Guest mode

Choose the guest option to get straight to recording purchases on this device.

Email and password

Create an account with your name, email address and password. Follow the verification email, and use the password reset option if needed.

Google sign-in

Choose Google on the sign-in screen and select the Google account you want to use. You can review account options in Docketo settings.

5. Backup and restore

Keep a copy you control.

Use the Backup section in Docketo settings to export your purchase records and attached receipt images. Choose password protection, save the backup somewhere reliable, and keep the password separately.

When moving to a new phone or recovering records, choose Restore in the same area and select your backup. Read the restore choice carefully: merging keeps existing records and adds the backup, while replacing swaps the current local records for the backup.

Backup checklist

  • Create a backup before changing phones
  • Back up after adding several important purchases
  • Use a unique password and do not lose it
  • Keep backups in secure storage you can access later
  • Test a restore only when you understand merge versus replace

Sync safely

  1. Sign in to your Docketo account.
  2. Make sure Premium is active.
  3. Open Settings and turn on Cloud Sync.
  4. Use Sync now after adding or changing records.
  5. On another signed-in device, turn on Cloud Sync and sync there too.

Cloud sync is an extra safeguard, not a replacement for keeping your own backup.

6. Sync across your devices

Optional cloud sync is a Premium feature.

Cloud Sync is for customers with an active Premium entitlement. It keeps eligible records and their changes available through your signed-in Docketo account, so you can access them on another device.

If a change does not appear, confirm you are signed into the same account, Cloud Sync is switched on, and the device has an internet connection. Then use Sync now.

Privacy and account care

You remain in control.

Review Docketo’s privacy choices in Settings, including analytics and crash-reporting controls where available. You can sign out, manage your account or request account deletion from the app and the website.

Need help?

Start with the FAQ for common questions. If you are stuck, include your phone model, Android version and a short description of what happened when you contact support. Never send passwords.
