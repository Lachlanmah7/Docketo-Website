What Docketo solves

Less searching. Less guessing.

Docketo is designed for the moment you need a receipt, want to check a warranty, or need to remember where and when something was purchased.

01

Keep purchase details together

Save the product name, brand, store, price, category, purchase date, serial number and notes in one record.

02

Attach receipt images

Store receipt and document images with the purchase instead of relying on email searches, paper folders or camera-roll scrolling.

03

Know when warranties end

Docketo calculates warranty status and can schedule local reminders so an expiry date does not quietly pass.

Local-first by design

Useful even without an account.

Docketo can be used locally on your phone. Purchase records and receipt images remain in app-controlled storage unless a future optional cloud feature is deliberately enabled.

  • Continue without creating an account
  • Store purchase records locally
  • Create encrypted backups
  • Delete local data from Settings
Current app status

What is active today

  • Local purchase and warranty storage
  • Email/password and Google sign-in
  • Local notifications
  • Backup and restore
  • AdMob banner for Free users
  • Premium subscription foundation

Cloud purchase sync and cloud receipt storage are planned Premium services and are not active in the current public-ready foundation.
