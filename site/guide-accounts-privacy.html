Accounts and privacy.

Use Docketo locally as a guest or sign in with email or Google when account-linked features are useful to you.

All guidesGetting started

Guest mode

Choose guest mode to use Docketo without an account. Your records stay on this device unless you create a backup or later use account-linked features.

Email account

Create an account with your name, email and password. Verify the email address when Docketo sends the verification message.

Google sign-in

Choose Google on the sign-in screen and select the account you want to use. Use the same account on another device if you intend to use Cloud Sync.

Email verification

Verify the address you control.

After creating an email account, open your email inbox and look for the Docketo verification email. Check the junk or spam folder if it is not visible. Follow the link in the email, then return to Docketo.

If the app offers a resend option, use it only after checking that your email address was typed correctly.

Forgot your password?

  1. Select the password-reset option from the sign-in screen.
  2. Enter the email address used for your Docketo account.
  3. Open the reset email and create a new password.
  4. Return to Docketo and sign in with the new password.

Account troubleshooting

  • Make sure you are choosing the original email or Google account.
  • Check internet access before signing in.
  • For email accounts, look in spam or junk for verification and reset messages.
  • Do not share a password, verification code or recovery link with anyone.
Protect your access

Use a strong password and keep it private.

Use a unique password for your email-based Docketo account. If you sign in with Google, your Google account’s security settings protect that login.

Privacy choices

Review controls in Settings.

Docketo provides settings for options such as analytics and crash reporting where available. These controls help you decide what diagnostic information can be used to improve the app.

For a full explanation of how information is handled, read the Docketo Privacy Policy.

Read Privacy Policy

Delete your account

If you decide to remove your Docketo account, first make a backup of any records you need. Then follow the account-deletion instructions in the app or on the website.

Account deletion is not the same as uninstalling the app. Use the dedicated deletion process when you want the account removed.
