Docketo Privacy Policy

Docketo is developed by Lachlan Maharjan in Australia. Privacy enquiries: lachlanmaharjan7@gmail.com.

Local-only use

You may use Docketo without creating an account. Purchase records, warranty details, notes, serial numbers, receipt images, notification schedules and settings remain in app-controlled storage on your device.

Account information

Docketo supports optional email/password and Google sign-in through Firebase Authentication. Depending on the sign-in method, account data may include your name, email, Firebase user ID, provider, profile image URL and email-verification status.

Purchase and receipt data

The current app does not upload purchase records or receipt images to cloud storage. Optional cloud sync may be added later and this policy will be updated before that occurs.

Backups

Backup export is initiated by the user. A backup may include purchases, warranty details, notes, serial numbers and receipt images. Users choose where it is saved.

Advertising

The Free version may display a Google AdMob dashboard banner. Google and advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, approximate location inferred from technical signals, consent choices and ad interactions.

Docketo does not intentionally send purchase names, prices, receipt content, notes, serial numbers or warranty details as custom advertising data.

Premium subscriptions

Docketo Premium may be offered through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes subscription and transaction information. Docketo may cache or store entitlement information so ads can be removed and Premium features unlocked.

Analytics and crash reporting

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics are not active in the current build.

Permissions

  • Camera for photographing receipts
  • Photos or media for selecting images
  • Notifications for warranty reminders
  • File selection for backup export and restore

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall Docketo. Firebase account information remains until the account is deleted, subject to limited security, legal and operational retention.

Children

Docketo is not directed to children under 13.
