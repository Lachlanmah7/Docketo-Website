Detailed guide

Understand Docketo Premium.

Premium gives you an ad-free dashboard and access to optional Cloud Sync for your signed-in account.

All guidesCloud Sync guide
What Premium includes

More focus and optional cloud access.

With an active Premium subscription, Docketo removes dashboard advertising and makes Cloud Sync available for your signed-in account. Cloud Sync helps you access account-linked records from another device.

Prices, billing period, trial availability and renewal details are shown by Google Play before you confirm a purchase. Read those details there, as they are the terms that apply to your purchase.

Premium at a glance

  • Ad-free dashboard while your subscription is active
  • Access to optional Cloud Sync
  • Subscription managed through Google Play
  • Use Restore purchases after reinstalling or changing phones

Subscribe

  1. Open Docketo Settings or the Premium area.
  2. Review the available Premium option.
  3. Select the subscription you want.
  4. Read the Google Play price and renewal information.
  5. Confirm your purchase through Google Play.
  6. Return to Docketo and wait for Premium to become active.
Buying Premium

Google Play handles the payment.

Docketo does not ask you to type payment details into the app. Google Play processes the subscription and shows the conditions before you approve it.

Restore purchases

Use this after a reinstall or new phone.

Install Docketo, sign in where needed and open the Premium settings. Choose Restore purchases. Use the same Google Play account that originally bought Premium. Once Google Play confirms the entitlement, Premium features should become available again.

If Restore purchases does not work

  • Check that you are using the original Google Play account.
  • Confirm the subscription is active in Google Play.
  • Check your internet connection.
  • Wait a moment, then try Restore purchases again.
  • Contact support with your order information—not your payment details.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed in Google Play, not from inside Docketo. Open Google Play, select your profile, then Payments & subscriptions and Subscriptions. Choose Docketo to view, change or cancel the subscription.

Cancelling stops future renewal. Your Premium access normally remains active until the end of the period already paid for; Google Play will show the applicable date.

Need help?

Keep records safe regardless.

Premium Cloud Sync is useful, but keep a password-protected Docketo backup of important records. It remains your independent copy when you change devices or need to troubleshoot.
