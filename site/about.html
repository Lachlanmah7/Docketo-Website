About Docketo

A practical idea built from a common problem.

Receipts disappear, warranties are forgotten, and important purchase details end up spread across paper folders, email inboxes, photo galleries and memory.

Docketo was created to give that information a clearer home. The aim is not to make purchase tracking complicated. It is to make the information useful when it is actually needed.

Developer

Docketo is developed by Lachlan Maharjan in Australia, with experience across software development, mobile applications, cloud services, application support and operational technology.

Product principles

  • Keep the core workflow simple
  • Allow local-only use
  • Give users control over backups and deletion
  • Introduce cloud services only as optional features
  • Be transparent about active and planned functionality
  • Improve based on testing and user feedback

Built with care

Docketo is being prepared for Google Play release through practical device testing, clear privacy information and ongoing feedback from early users.
