Docketo features

Built around the full life of a purchase.

From the day something is bought to the day its warranty expires, Docketo keeps the information connected and easier to find.

P

Purchase records

Record product name, brand, store, price, category, date, warranty duration, serial number and notes.

R

Receipt images

Attach multiple receipt or supporting-document images to a purchase and view them later in a full-screen viewer.

W

Warranty tracking

See whether a warranty is active, expiring soon or expired. Warranty length can be entered flexibly.

N

Local reminders

Schedule warranty reminders on the device. Tapping a notification can open the relevant purchase record.

B

Backup and restore

Create portable backups that include records and receipt images. Password protection is recommended.

S

Search and sorting

Search saved products, filter by warranty state and sort records to find what matters faster.

Dashboard

A quick view of what you own.

The dashboard summarises the number of products, active warranties, expiring warranties, expired warranties and total recorded purchase value. Recent purchases remain close at hand.

  • Product count
  • Warranty summaries
  • Total purchase value
  • Recent-purchase shortcuts

Example use cases

  • Finding the receipt for a faulty appliance
  • Checking whether headphones are still under warranty
  • Keeping serial numbers for electronics
  • Preparing an inventory for insurance purposes
  • Moving records to a new phone using a backup
Account options

Use Docketo your way.

An account is optional for local use, but sign-in supports identity, verification and future account-linked Premium services.

Guest mode

Continue without an account and keep records local to the device.

Email account

Create an account with a name, email and password, then verify the email address.

Google sign-in

Use an eligible Google account for a simpler sign-in experience.
