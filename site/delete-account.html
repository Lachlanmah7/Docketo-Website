Account and data deletion

Delete your Docketo account.

Signed-in users can request account deletion directly in Docketo:

Docketo → Settings → Account → Delete account

What account deletion removes

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • Your Docketo sign-in identity and authentication profile
  • Cloud-synchronised data associated with your account, where applicable

Local data is separate

Deleting the authentication account does not automatically delete purchases, receipt images, warranty records or backup files stored locally.

To remove local information: Docketo → Settings → Delete all local data

Request deletion without the app

Email lachlanmaharjan7@gmail.com with the subject Docketo account deletion request. Include the email used for the Docketo account. Identity verification may be required.

Retention

Limited information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or service operation.
