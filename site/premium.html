How billing works

One Premium entitlement, flexible billing.

Docketo is designed to offer monthly and annual subscription options through Google Play. Prices, billing periods and renewal information are displayed by Google Play.

  • Monthly base plan
  • Annual base plan
  • Automatic renewal through Google Play
  • Restore purchases
  • Manage or cancel through Google Play

Premium account requirement

A signed-in Docketo account is required before purchasing Premium. This helps associate future account-linked services with the correct user.

The Free version remains available even when a user does not subscribe.
