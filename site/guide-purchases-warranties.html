Purchases, receipts and warranties.

Build a record that is ready for a return, repair, warranty claim or insurance question.

All guidesNext: protect your records
Add a purchase

Record the facts you may need later.

Open the purchase list and choose the option to add a purchase. Enter the product name, then add the store, brand, price and purchase date. Use category to make browsing easier, and add the serial number for products that have one.

Use the notes field for details you would otherwise forget: return deadline, colour, model, repair contact or a brief description of the issue.

Keep product names searchable

Use “Sony WH-1000XM5 headphones” rather than “Headphones”. Include the model where it matters. It makes search, warranty claims and backup recovery much easier.

Add a receipt or document

  1. Open the purchase you are creating or editing.
  2. Choose the receipt or image option.
  3. Take a photo or select an existing image.
  4. Check that the store name, date and total are readable.
  5. Save the purchase.

You can add supporting images too, such as an invoice, serial-number label or repair paperwork.

Receipts

Keep proof beside the product.

Paper fades and photos get lost. Attach the receipt while you are adding the purchase so it stays with the warranty information. Later, open the saved product to view its images in the app.

Warranty tracking

Tell Docketo how long you are covered.

Add the warranty period in the purchase form. Docketo uses the purchase date and warranty length to show whether the item is active, expiring soon or expired.

Use the warranty filters in your list when you want to focus on items that need attention. Update the record if the retailer replaces the item or extends the warranty.

Before you rely on a warranty

  • Keep the receipt image clear and complete
  • Save the serial number when available
  • Check the exact warranty terms from the seller or manufacturer
  • Do not wait until the final day to contact support

Set a reminder

  1. Open the purchase record.
  2. Choose the warranty reminder option.
  3. Select a date before the warranty ends.
  4. Allow Docketo notifications when your phone asks.
  5. Use the notification to open the relevant purchase when it arrives.
Reminders

Give yourself enough time to act.

A reminder is useful for expensive items, devices that are starting to fail, or any product with a short warranty. If you do not receive one, check that notifications are enabled for Docketo in your phone settings and use the in-app notification test if available.

Keep it safe

Your records are valuable—back them up.

A backup protects your records and receipt images when you change or lose a device.
