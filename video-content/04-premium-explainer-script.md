# Video 4 — Docketo Free and Premium

**Recommended length:** 60–90 seconds

## Script

Docketo Free includes the core tools for recording purchases, storing receipt images, tracking warranties, scheduling local reminders and creating backups.

The Free version may display a small dashboard banner.

Docketo Premium removes advertising while the subscription is active.

Premium is also intended to support advanced services as Docketo grows, including optional cloud sync, multi-device access and advanced reporting.

Those cloud services are planned and should only be described as available after they are released.

Subscriptions are managed through Google Play, and the Free version remains usable without a subscription.
