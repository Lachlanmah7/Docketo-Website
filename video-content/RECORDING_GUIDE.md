# Docketo Video Recording Guide

## Recommended tools

- Android built-in screen recorder
- OBS Studio for desktop editing
- CapCut, DaVinci Resolve or Clipchamp for editing
- YouTube Studio for upload and captions

## Recording preparation

1. Use sample purchase data only.
2. Do not display real email addresses, receipts, addresses, card details or serial numbers.
3. Enable Do Not Disturb.
4. Remove personal notifications from the status bar.
5. Use a clean test account.
6. Record at 1080p where possible.
7. Use slow, deliberate taps.

## Brand style

- Calm and practical
- Avoid exaggerated claims
- Show real app screens
- Clearly label planned features
- Use the Docketo blue logo
- Keep videos short and task-focused

## Website embedding

After a YouTube upload, use:

```html
<iframe
  width="560"
  height="315"
  src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/YOUTUBE_VIDEO_ID"
  title="Docketo video"
  frameborder="0"
  allowfullscreen>
</iframe>
```
