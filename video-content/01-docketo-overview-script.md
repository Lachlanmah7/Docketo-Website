# Video 1 — Docketo Overview

**Recommended length:** 60–90 seconds  
**Format:** YouTube Short or standard landscape video

## Opening

**Screen:** Docketo logo, then dashboard.

**Voiceover:**

Keeping a receipt should not mean searching through emails, paper folders and hundreds of photos.

Docketo gives your purchases, receipts and warranties one organised home.

## Middle

**Screen:** Add Purchase form.

Record the product, store, price, purchase date, warranty length, serial number and notes.

**Screen:** Attach receipt images.

Add receipt or document images directly to the purchase, so they are there when you need them.

**Screen:** Dashboard warranty cards.

Docketo tracks active, expiring and expired warranties, and can remind you before important dates are missed.

**Screen:** Backup settings.

Your purchase data can remain local on your phone, and you can create a backup when you choose.

## Ending

**Screen:** Docketo logo and website address.

Docketo. Receipts. Warranties. Sorted.

## YouTube title

Docketo App Overview — Organise Purchases, Receipts and Warranties

## Description

Docketo helps you record purchases, keep receipt images, track warranties, schedule reminders and create backups.

Learn more at your Docketo website.
