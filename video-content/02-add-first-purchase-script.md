# Video 2 — Add Your First Purchase

**Recommended length:** 2–3 minutes  
**Format:** Landscape screen recording with voiceover

## Introduction

In this video, I will show you how to add a purchase in Docketo and attach a receipt.

## Steps

1. Open Docketo and tap **Add Purchase**.
2. Enter the product name.
3. Select or enter the category.
4. Add the brand and store.
5. Enter the purchase price and date.
6. Add the warranty length using the available duration controls.
7. Enter a serial number if the product has one.
8. Add any notes you may need later.
9. Use Camera or Gallery to attach the receipt.
10. Review the details and save.

## Explain the result

After saving, the purchase appears on the dashboard and in All Products.

Docketo calculates the warranty status from the purchase date and warranty duration.

The receipt image stays linked to that purchase and can be opened in the full-screen viewer.

## Closing

That is all it takes to create a complete purchase record in Docketo.
